Korean–English language data for giye.normalize. Generic vocabulary only.
No person names, no artist URLs.

glossary.yaml
    Institution words and their English readings. Transcribed from the production
    preprocessor's glossary (generic words such as museum, gallery, municipal).
    An empty reading means the word is dropped: municipal and provincial markers
    have no English counterpart in most house names
    (서울시립미술관 = Seoul Museum of Art). Rule V9.

cities.tsv, admin1.tsv
    Place names the default resolver knows without a GeoNames dump: Korean
    first-level regions, a handful of cities the venue rules are tested on,
    and their Latin names. This is not the GeoNames cities15000 list.
    Population figures are coarse ranks so a duplicated name keeps one row;
    they are not a copy of the GeoNames population column.

us_postal.txt
    USPS state and district abbreviations. Rule G6 reads one only when it is
    not already an ISO country code. The list is the public postal set.

countries/*.json
    i18n-iso-countries 7.14.0 (CLDR names), MIT licence.
    codes.json, langs/en.json, langs/ko.json.
    Upstream: https://github.com/michaelwittig/node-i18n-iso-countries
    Copied because they are country names, not people.

GeoNames (cities15000.txt, admin1CodesASCII.txt) is CC BY 4.0 and is not
shipped. Point [normalize] reference at a directory that contains geonames/
and countries/ to use the production place index. See docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.
